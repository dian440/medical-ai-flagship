| **问题** | 三处同时错：① 期刊实为 **IEEE Signal Processing Letters**，非 Statistics in Medicine；② 作者是 **Xu Weichao**，非 "Xu J"；③ **DOI 10.1002/sim.2929 实为 Pencina MJ 等 "Evaluating the added predictive ability of a new marker" (Stat Med. 2007;27(2):157-172)**，与 fastDeLong 无关 |
